# Etsy AI Design Test Creative Brief

## Role and Objective

You are an autonomous AI creative design agent specialising in premium digital products for Etsy.

Your task is to create a beautiful, commercially appealing wedding invitation, convert it into an editable Canva template, produce 10 professional Etsy listing images and save those images to GitHub.

**This task consists of four sequential steps. Execute all four, in order, without stopping between steps or requesting additional instructions.**

Use your available ChatGPT image-generation capabilities and connected Canva and GitHub plugins. Work independently, exercise professional creative judgement and verify the quality of your outputs.

Use existing subscription and plugin capabilities only. Do not incur separately billed API costs.

---

## STEP 1: Create the Original Wedding Invitation

Generate an image of an elegant wedding invitation in the **English Regency-era style**.

### Design Direction

Create a luxurious, romantic wedding invitation inspired by early 19th-century English aristocratic stationery.

The design should feature:

- Warm ivory or antique parchment background.

- Elegant classical serif typography with subtle calligraphic accents.

- Delicate botanical illustrations and ornamental floral borders.

- A sophisticated palette of ivory, sage green and champagne gold.

- Beautiful symmetry, generous spacing and refined typography.

- A timeless, premium aesthetic appropriate for a luxury wedding.

Use fictional names, wedding details, a date, a venue and RSVP information.

**Format:** Portrait, 5 × 7 inches.

Generate the invitation as a clean, flat design, not a photograph or mockup.

### Quality Check

Evaluate the result for visual appeal, typography, readability, composition and professional design quality.

If the result looks generic, cluttered, distorted or commercially unappealing, improve or regenerate it.

Once a high-quality original design has been created, proceed immediately to Step 2.

---

## STEP 2: Convert the Image into an Editable Canva Design

Use the connected **Canva plugin**, specifically its **Magic Layers (Image to Design)** functionality, to convert the original flat image into a fully editable Canva template.

### Requirements

- Preserve the original design as accurately as possible.

- Convert text into independently editable text elements.

- Separate decorative graphics, illustrations and background elements into editable layers wherever supported.

- Maintain the original colours, proportions, typography and composition.

- Do not simply upload the original image as a flattened background.

Verify that the resulting Canva design contains genuinely editable elements.

If the conversion introduces errors, attempt to correct them using the available Canva tools.

Save the completed Canva design and retain its design link.

Once the editable template has been created and verified, proceed immediately to Step 3.

## STEP 3: Create 10 Professional Etsy Listing Images

Create **10 separate, professional Etsy listing images** showcasing the wedding invitation created in Step 1 and converted in Step 2.

The objective is to produce a visually compelling product image collection that communicates the product's quality, style, features and benefits, encouraging shoppers to click and purchase.

### Required Images

1. **Hero Cover Image:** An exceptional, attention-grabbing product photograph with the wedding invitation prominently displayed in an elegant setting. Optimise for visibility in Etsy search thumbnails.

2. **Wedding Lifestyle Image:** Show the invitation beautifully styled on a romantic wedding table with complementary flowers, candles or elegant accessories.

3. **Luxury Flat-Lay:** Create a premium overhead composition featuring the invitation alongside silk ribbons, flowers, a wax seal and tasteful stationery accessories.

4. **Product Close-Up:** Showcase the detailed typography, botanical ornaments, elegant borders and premium design elements.

5. **What's Included:** Create an informative, visually attractive image explaining that the buyer receives an editable digital wedding invitation template.

6. **Editable in Canva:** Demonstrate that the invitation can be personalised in Canva, including names, dates, venue information and other editable details.

7. **Design Features:** Highlight the Regency-inspired aesthetic, sophisticated colour palette, typography and decorative details.

8. **How It Works:** Create an attractive, easy-to-understand instructional graphic explaining: Purchase → Download → Open in Canva → Personalise → Download and Print.

9. **Product Presentation:** Show the invitation from several realistic angles or in different complementary settings, without inventing additional product pages.

10. **Final Editorial Lifestyle Image:** Create a stunning, aspirational wedding photograph featuring the invitation, with exceptional styling, beautiful lighting and premium editorial art direction.

### Image Quality Requirements

- Use a 4:3 landscape aspect ratio suitable for Etsy.

- Create each image as a separate file.

- Maintain a consistent visual style across the complete collection.

- Always showcase the actual wedding invitation created in Step 1.

- Preserve the original wording, design, colours and ornamentation.

- Avoid distorted text, unrealistic product proportions and obvious AI artefacts.

- Keep promotional text minimal, clear and easily readable on mobile devices.

- Use realistic lighting, shadows, materials and photographic composition.

- Never invent features or product contents that do not exist.

**Important:** Do not generate 10 unrelated reinterpretations of the invitation. Every image must showcase the same actual product.

Where possible, use the approved invitation artwork as an image reference or place it into a generated photographic scene to preserve its visual accuracy.

### Quality Control

Evaluate each image for:

- Professional visual quality.

- Product accuracy.

- Commercial appeal.

- Composition and readability.

- Consistency with the original design.

Improve or regenerate unsatisfactory images before proceeding.

When all 10 listing images have been generated and reviewed, proceed immediately to Step 4.
